# Dilanka Express: interior-page design references

Reviewed 13 October 2026. These are selected references for particular interaction and layout strengths, not an objective ranking of booking companies. The resulting layouts, colours and copy are original to the Dilanka preview. Competitor fares, company claims, policies, photography and passenger information were not imported.

| Dilanka page | Reference | Useful pattern and application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routes | [FlixBus routes](https://global.flixbus.com/bus-routes), [Vonlane terminals](https://vonlane.com/index/terminals) | Destination discovery and clear stop information. Dilanka uses photographic corridor cards, town search, road-type filters and expandable maps with a stop list. Approximate corridors and unconfirmed boarding details are labelled. |
| Fleet | [Vonlane gallery](https://vonlane.com/index/gallery), [Vonlane seat layouts](https://vonlane.com/index/about#layout) | Make the coach tangible through photography, video and seat diagrams. Dilanka has large fleet profiles, numbered expandable 49/51/53-seat templates, amenities from the configured vehicle, and the existing company photo/video gallery. Images are fleet references, not verified departure assignments. |
| Company story | [Why Vonlane](https://vonlane.com/index/about) | Give the operator a distinct personality and explain the passenger experience. Dilanka uses authentic depot/team photography, the sourced Ampara company description, and a three-part explanation of the prototype booking experience. No invented company history, awards or service statistics. |
| Contact | [National Express contact and support](https://www.nationalexpress.com/en/help/contact) | Make support paths easy to distinguish. Dilanka prioritises the published hotline, an alternative number, email, address search and booking help. WhatsApp is a user-initiated external link. No fake submission form or unsupported response-time promise. |
| Help & policies | [National Express help](https://www.nationalexpress.com/en/help/contact), [Magiya booking guide](https://magiya.lk/need-help) | Organise assistance around passenger tasks. Dilanka adds question search, Booking/Tickets/Travel filters, native expandable answers, useful shortcuts and a privacy section. Policy values continue to come from the current catalogue rather than duplicated fixed rules. |
| Departure search | [Magiya booking guide](https://magiya.lk/need-help), the Nuwantha screenshots supplied by the user | Compare bus, departure, availability and fare before choosing a seat. Dilanka retains working date, layout, time and fare controls and route maps; improves spacing and typography, and displays configured amenities. The original passenger flow remains intact. |
| Seat selection, passenger details & review | [Magiya step-by-step booking](https://magiya.lk/need-help), [FlixBus service](https://www.flixbus.com/service) | Numbered seats, clear stop choices and an itemised total before confirmation. Dilanka uses a three-stage progress strip, coach photo in the desktop journey summary, persistent total on mobile, real seat holds and explicit no-payment confirmation. No OTP or login added. |
| Find my ticket | [FlixBus Manage My Booking](https://shop.flixbus.com/rebooking/login), linked from [FlixBus service](https://www.flixbus.com/service) | Keep booking retrieval focused. Dilanka uses a two-column image/form composition and retains its existing reference-plus-private-code protection. Its access mechanism is custom to this prototype. |
| Confirmation & QR ticket | [FlixBus app and digital ticket](https://www.flixbus.com/service/bus-app) | Keep the ticket close and usable on a phone. Dilanka preserves its working per-seat signed QR, PDF download and private booking recovery; improves the boarding-pass hierarchy and keeps passenger, seat, date and boarding time easy to scan. |
| Public QR verification | [TerraOS operations and driver app](https://www.terramobility.com/platform/operations) | Clear ticket status and deliberate staff validation. The Dilanka public status card is a custom design; viewing it does not check anyone in or expose passenger contact details. Existing repeat-use and wrong-departure checks remain unchanged. |
| Staff login & operations | [TerraOS operations](https://www.terramobility.com/platform/operations) | Separate departures, seats, manifests and check-in into recognisable operational tasks. Dilanka keeps its implemented functions and roles, with a forest-green sidebar, restrained metrics, quieter tables and responsive tab navigation. TerraOS's public product examples were reviewed; no restricted console was accessed. |

## Visual direction

Ivory backgrounds, deep forest green, muted gold details, Georgia editorial headings, and DM Sans forms/data. Split photographic page introductions are followed by practical page-specific content. Checkout and staff data retain readable task-oriented hierarchy. Mobile layouts stack image/text compositions, preserve seat geometry and contain wide tables inside their own horizontal scrolling regions.

## Media and research limitations

Reused approved prototype assets already documented in [LANDING_DESIGN.md](./LANDING_DESIGN.md) and [MEDIA_AND_MAPS.md](./MEDIA_AND_MAPS.md). Route photo credits are available in an expandable credit section. The client-provided fleet photo remains original on the fleet and interior pages; the enhanced version remains on the landing page.

Vonlane gallery/about were inspected in the browser as well as through public page text. Other references were reviewed through their official public pages and guides. Nuwantha could not be fetched through the research tool in this pass, and Tripimate exposed only its JavaScript shell there; this pass therefore makes no claim to have fully audited those two live booking systems. Previously supplied screenshots and existing project research remain local context. National Express's direct ticket/manage-booking URLs redirected to its international retailer, so the usable contact/help page was used as the support reference instead.

This work does not verify Dilanka's upcoming fares, schedules, registrations, exact boarding points or approved policies. It does not add payments, delivery integrations, live tracking or a public deployment.
