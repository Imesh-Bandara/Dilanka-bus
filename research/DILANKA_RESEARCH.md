## What can now inform the prototype

Use actual company identity, public contacts, DE branding and collected fleet media. Use published route names and stop corridors as reference, retaining a demo notice. Use the NE-3230 numbered layout only for that bus or a clearly labelled demo bus based on it. Store source date and approval status with seed data. Build separate route variants rather than merging every Ampara–Colombo service into one route.

Still obtain from staff: approved current trip roster and vehicle assignments; complete fare matrix and fees; layouts for other buses; exact boarding locations; booking cutoff; child/luggage/cancellation rules; and preferred original media files. Findings reduce the missing information but do not establish a complete production-ready operating dataset.
